26 August 2026 · Rasuwa, Nepal

The alert went out 38 minutes after the mountain fell

At 08:37 a mass of ice and rock broke off the north flank of Langtang Lirung and dropped about 1,200 m into the Lhende Khola. The flood ran down the Bhote Koshi and the Trishuli. At least 1,454 people died in Nepal and more than 5,000 are missing.

20 June to 19 August

Radar watched the slope creep for seven weeks

NISAR passes every 12 days. Each pair of passes gives a map of how far the ground moved in between. On the map, coloured rings are moving ground, and tighter rings mean faster motion.

In the last window the source slope moved 0.96 m in 24 days, about 4 cm a day. The two mid-July windows were lost: wet monsoon snow scrambles the signal. The rate never sped up, so the creep alone was no alarm.
